The patient journey

From a WhatsApp message
to a return visit.

Maya wants a cleaning this week. Here’s how reception books her visit, fills the slot she gives up and follows up after she leaves.

01 Enquiry02 Offer03 Book04 Move05 Attend06 Follow up07 Recall

01 / Enquiry

Give every enquiry a next step.

Maya asks for a cleaning on WhatsApp. Messages, website enquiries and missed calls go into one queue, with the branch and the person responsible for replying on each record.

02 / Offer

Offer a time without the back-and-forth.

The booking agent checks available times and Maya’s contact preferences, then drafts a reply for reception to approve. Clinical questions go straight to the care team.

03 / Book

Confirm the slot in the calendar.

Maya picks Thursday at 3:30. Once the calendar confirms the booking, reception sees it on her record.

04 / Move

Move one visit. Fill the opening.

Maya asks for Friday. Once her new time is confirmed, reception can offer her old slot to a patient who asked to come in sooner.

05 / Attend

Follow up after the visit.

Reception marks Maya as attended. The aftercare agent prepares the clinic’s approved note, and any treatment questions go back to her care team.

06 / Follow up

Make it easy to leave an honest review.

Send the same optional review invitation to every patient who attended and agreed to follow-up. How they rate the visit doesn’t change who gets an invitation.

07 / Recall

Bring patients back when they’re due.

Top Smile sets Maya’s next visit date. If she’s due, has agreed to reminders and hasn’t booked, the recall agent prepares a message.

Getting started

Do we need new booking software?

The plan starts with the calendars you already use. The written scope shows which booking actions each system supports and which ones stay with reception.

Can we keep our WhatsApp and website?

We start with your existing WhatsApp and website. Phone is a separate decision: you can start with a missed-call queue and decide whether voice automation belongs in scope.

Can branches keep different rules?

Yes. Oud Metha and Dubai Silicon Oasis have separate hours, booking rules and staff access. Managers can compare both branches from one view, with the permissions you set.

When do we get a quote?

Once we know the booking software at each branch, we send a scope with a timeline and separate costs for setup, support and any usage.

Bookings and reminders

What happens to existing bookings?

They stay in your calendar. We test bookings, changes and cancellations separately, then agree when to turn on each workflow.

Can patients reschedule in WhatsApp?

Where the calendar supports changes, patients can pick a new time in the same conversation. Their original appointment stays in place until the new one is confirmed.

How do you handle cancellations and no-shows?

Once a slot opens, offer it to a patient on the waitlist. It’s booked when the calendar confirms it. For missed visits, follow your branch’s rebooking rules.

Who receives reminders?

Your team sets the due dates and approves the wording. Reminders go to patients who agreed to receive them and are still due. They stop when a patient books or opts out.

Patient care and data

Who answers treatment questions?

Your clinicians. Agents use the aftercare wording and recall dates your team approves. Treatment questions go straight to the care team.

Who can see patient records?

We agree which details the workflow needs, who on your team can access them, and which services may process them. We confirm those rules with you before launch.

What happens to our data if we stop?

Appointments remain in your booking system. The written agreement lists which records you can export and how your data is handed back and removed.

How do payments and insurance work?

Payment links can use your existing provider. The record updates after that provider confirms payment. Insurance checks are scoped separately by payer or clinic system; eligibility is separate from treatment coverage.
